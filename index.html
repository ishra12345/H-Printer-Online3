Safety Notice: Always switch off and unplug your printer before clearing internal rollers.

  1. Turn off the printer power switch and disconnect the power cord.
  2. Open the front door, main tray, and rear duplex hatch carefully.
  3. Grasp the jammed paper with both hands and pull gently in the direction of the paper feed path to avoid tearing sheets.
  4. Check rollers for micro paper shreds using a light source. Clean rubber pickup rollers with a damp lint-free cloth.
  5. Reload fresh, uncurled A4/Letter paper and align the tray edge guides snug against the paper stack.

Faded or streaky prints are usually caused by dry ink deposits in nozzle orifices.

  • Run Printhead Nozzle Check: Access your printer maintenance software and initiate a nozzle pattern test.
  • Execute Head Cleaning: Perform 1-2 standard head cleaning cycles. Avoid running more than 3 consecutive deep cleans to prevent ink waste tank overflow.
  • Manual Contact Cleaning: For removable cartridges, wipe electrical copper contacts with a lint-free swab dipped in distilled water.

  2. Reset Print Spooler: On Windows, press Win + R, type services.msc, right-click Print Spooler, and choose Restart.
  3. Assign a static IP address to your printer via router administration page to prevent dynamic IP lease changes.

Outdated drivers can cause print jobs to hang indefinitely in spooling status.

  • Uninstall generic OS drivers and download manufacturer-specific full software packages.
  • Ensure your USB data cable is under 6 feet long if performing a wired driver setup.
  • Never interrupt power supply during a printer firmware flash update.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions regarding printer configuration.

First, ensure the printer is turned on and connected to the same Wi-Fi network or USB cable as your computer. Go to Settings > Devices > Printers & Scanners on your computer, select your printer, and ensure "Use Printer Offline" is unchecked in the queue settings. Restarting both router and printer often resolves this issue immediately.
Always download drivers directly from your printer manufacturer’s official page by searching your exact model number. Make sure to choose the correct operating system version (e.g., Windows 11, macOS Sonoma).
Ensure your Wi-Fi frequency is set to 2.4 GHz as many printers do not 5 GHz networks. Re-enter the Wi-Fi password on the printer control panel or use WPS button connection if supported.
